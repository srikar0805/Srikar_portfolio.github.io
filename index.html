About

I build machine learning systems and the measurement that tells you whether to trust them.

Right now that is infrastructure at the University of Missouri's Precision and Automated Agriculture Lab: I own the training pipeline, the evaluation framework and the data quality tooling. The most useful thing I did there was not a model change. I audited our training data across eight rounds and found 250 mislabelled records, and the ranking of our models changed once they were fixed.

Before graduate school I spent 13 months at MAQ Software, a Microsoft partner, delivering production systems for Fortune 500 clients. Half that role was analysis rather than code: gathering requirements from business stakeholders and writing the specifications developers built from.

Outside coursework I build LLM tooling and ship it. All public, all with tests and CI.
